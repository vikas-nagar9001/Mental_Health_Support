Help & Support

Find answers about MindCare, privacy, and campus care pathways.

Is my data shared with faculty?

No. Wellness reflections and chat history are not linked to academic transcripts. See Settings → Privacy for controls.

Is the AI a therapist?

No. AI Support is a coping companion, not clinical care. For counseling, book a campus counselor.

How do I book counseling?

Open Counseling from the sidebar, pick a counselor, choose a slot, and confirm.
